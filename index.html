Our Story

A corner of
San Sebastián
in the Winelands

Chef David Schneider built Cantina around a single, powerful idea: that the most memorable meals happen when barriers fall away — between kitchen and table, between nations of flavour, between strangers who share a bottle.

Centred on an eye-catching communal table of bright lemon tiles, with an evocative soundtrack and a menu that roams freely between Basque coast and Cape terroir, Cantina is an ode to the original Chefs Warehouse spirit — generous, vibrant, and entirely alive.

The
Menu

All dishes are designed for the table. Order generously. Share freely. The paella feeds the whole room.

Bright, precise, and made to open the palate — our cold plates channel the pintxos bars of the Basque coast.

Signature Cold Plate

Bluefin Tuna Belly

Silky tuna belly, slow-cured and thinly sliced, served with braised artichoke hearts, preserved lemon oil, micro herbs, and a touch of aged sherry vinegar. Each plate a small act of restraint. Each bite anything but.

Raw Gluten-free Signature

Tortilla Española

Potato · Manchego · Caramelised Onion

The classic reinterpreted — layers of slow-cooked potato bound in egg, finished with oozy manchego and a golden caramelised onion jam. A dish that demands to be shared in thick, generous slices.

VegetarianHouse Classic

Grilled Sardines on Toast

Sourdough · Romesco · Pickled Shallot

Whole sardines charred over coals, served on grilled sourdough with house-made romesco, bright pickled shallots, and a drizzle of Arbequina olive oil. Sea and smoke in every bite.

From the Grill

Ibérico Charcuterie

Jamón · Chorizo · Seasonal Accompaniments

A curated board of cured meats: hand-carved jamón ibérico, air-dried chorizo, cornichons, Marcona almonds, honeycomb, and house-baked pan de cristal.

Today's Pick

Cooked with heat, patience, and a deep respect for Basque tradition — our hot plates are the warm heart of Cantina.

Gambas al Ajillo

Garlic Prawns · Chilli · Fino Sherry

Whole prawns sizzled furiously in terra cotta with fragrant garlic, dried chilli, and a glug of good fino sherry. The oil at the bottom is the best part — don't waste a drop. Crusty bread essential.

SeafoodHouse Favourite

Fire-Grilled Mushrooms

Seasonal Mushrooms · Egg Yolk · Truffle

A generous tangle of seasonal wild mushrooms blistered over live fire, finished with a slow-cooked egg yolk, aged parmesan, and a thread of truffle oil. Earthy, rich, and deeply satisfying.

VegetarianSeasonal

Patatas Bravas

Twice-Cooked Potato · Spiced Sauce · Aioli

Crispy on the outside, fluffy within — twice-cooked potato batons served with a fiery bravas sauce and house-made saffron aioli. The most honest thing on the menu. Also the most addictive.

VegetarianSharing

Pimientos de Padrón

Blistered Peppers · Fleur de Sel

The game of Padrón peppers: nine mild, one fiercely hot — impossible to tell apart until it's too late. Blistered in olive oil and finished with coarse sea salt. Simple. Electric. Worth the risk.

VeganSpanish Classic

Larger plates built for appetite — fired over coals, rested with care, and served with the confidence of a kitchen that knows exactly what it's doing.

Signature Fire Plate

700g Bone-In Ribeye

A massive bone-in ribeye, dry-aged and cooked directly over live coals in the Basque tradition. Rested, carved tableside, and served with chimichurri verde, roasted bone marrow, and fire-charred lemon. This is a meal that demands red wine, good company, and absolutely no hurry.

For Sharing Live Fire Signature

Carne Asada

Fire-Grilled Beef · Chimichurri Verde

Skirt steak marinated in herbs, garlic, and citrus, then fired fast over hot coals. Sliced against the grain and draped in vibrant chimichurri verde. The simplest thing on the menu. Often the first to disappear.

Live Fire

Whole Grilled Sea Bream

Sea Bream · Salsa Verde · Grilled Lemon

A whole line-caught sea bream, scored and roasted over the open fire until the skin blisters and crisps. Served with a bright, herb-heavy salsa verde and charred lemon. Bones and all — that's the point.

SustainableWhole Fish

The centrepiece. The reason to gather. Our paellas are made for the table — to be passed around, argued over, and finished completely. Allow 35 minutes. Worth every second.

The Ritual · Serves 2–4

Paella Cantina

Saffron-stained bomba rice cooked slowly in a rich seafood caldo until it develops the prized socarrat crust at the base. Topped with whole prawns, mussels, calamari, and roasted piquillo peppers. A shared experience. Not a side dish. Please allow 35 minutes — good things take time.

Serves 2–4 35 Mins The Centrepiece Gluten-free

Paella Negra

Squid Ink · Calamari · Aioli

Our darkest paella — bomba rice stained jet-black with squid ink, loaded with tender calamari and finished with a golden saffron aioli that cuts through the brine. Dramatic. Delicious. Turns your smile temporarily very dark.

Paella Vegetariana

Seasonal Vegetables · Saffron · Smoked Paprika

For those who take vegetables seriously. Seasonal greens, roasted root vegetables, and charred peppers in a rich, smoky saffron rice. The socarrat is the same. The satisfaction is absolute.

Small, honest, and precisely sweet — our desserts respect the appetite of guests who have already eaten well.

House Classic

Crema Catalana

Silky vanilla custard, torched at the table until the sugar shatters. The sound alone is worth the order.

From the Pastry Kitchen

Churros con Chocolate

Crisp, hot churros dusted in cinnamon sugar with a thick, dark Spanish-style drinking chocolate. Childlike joy in an elegant setting.

Seasonal

Turron Ice Cream Sandwich

House-churned turron ice cream sandwiched between two thin almond wafers. Seasonal accompaniments. Deceptively simple.
